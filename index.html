Course materials are based on Unit 1 of 普通高中教科书 英语 选择性必修 第二册 (外语教学与研究出版社) and are intended for classroom use. Dictionary examples are quoted from the Oxford Advanced Learner’s Dictionary and the Cambridge Advanced Learner’s Dictionary, with links to each entry. Word audio: Youdao Dictionary. Lesson 2 video: FLTRP, hosted on Bilibili. Lesson 3 video lecture: 爱学英语的Vivian, hosted on Bilibili. Recordings of the poem and the passage: synthetic voices made with Kokoro (Apache 2.0). Chinese translations of the poem and the passage were prepared for this course. Background notes and sentence notes in Teacher Resources: the teacher’s book for this unit (外语教学与研究出版社).
